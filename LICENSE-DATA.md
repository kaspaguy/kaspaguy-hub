## Not covered: quotes

Some fields quote a project describing itself (for example `says` in `data/lookalikes.json`). Those words belong to
their authors. We quote short excerpts only so readers can tell projects apart, and CC BY 4.0 does not cover them.
Every quote records its source (`says_source`) and the date it was copied (`says_date`), and the build refuses a quote
without both.
