## 1. Privacy policy (KaspaChat testnet)

KaspaChat is a private messenger on Kaspa, a project by Kaspaguy. This page says what we collect during the testnet,
why, and for how long. Privacy questions: support@kaspachat.com. Testnet feedback and bug reports: testnet@kaspachat.com. You can ask us to delete anything you sent us sooner by emailing support@kaspachat.com.

**Your messages.** Chats are end-to-end encrypted: only the people in a chat can read them, not us. Our servers only pass
encrypted messages along and keep no copy after 7 days, and none is ever in a backup. Keys never leave your device.

**Signing up.** No phone number, no email, no card. Your free name is random and made from your own key.

**IP addresses.** Desktop and Android use built-in Tor, so our servers never see your IP. Otherwise your IP is held in
memory for spam limits only and wiped within 10 minutes. We keep no access logs for the app.

**Anonymous credits.** Messages are paid with anonymous tokens that can't be linked to your account, by design.

**What we do keep, and for how long**

| What | Why | How long |
|---|---|---|
| Feedback you send (in-app "Send feedback" or testnet@kaspachat.com), including the optional contact and "where did you hear about us" answer | To fix bugs and reply to you | Deleted after 30 days |
| Payout records for bounties: amount, date, the KAS address you gave, report reference | To pay bounties and keep a record of payments | Kept as a payment record for accounting and dispute resolution |
| Questions asked to the help bot in our Telegram group that it couldn't answer, with addresses, emails, phone numbers, @handles and links removed | To improve the FAQ | Deleted after 30 days, or sooner once used |
| Daily counts of visits per link source (for example "from X: 12") | To see which channels work | Totals only: no IP, cookie or device data is stored |
| Hashed account IDs in daily snapshots | To count aggregate usage during the campaign | 14 days; see below |

*During the testnet campaign we process hashed account IDs in daily snapshots for 14 days to count aggregate usage. The
key and the snapshots are deleted on day 15.*

**Telegram.** If you join our Telegram channel or group, Telegram shows us your membership like any group admin sees it.
Our help bot reads messages in the public group only to answer them, in memory; it stores no usernames, Telegram IDs or
IPs, only the question category and whether it answered.

**No tracking.** No analytics cookies, no ad trackers, no email list. kaspaguy.com logs page views without IP addresses.

**Testnet.** This policy covers the testnet. Mainnet will have its own policy, published before launch.

- KaspaChat's testnet runs on Kaspa testnet-10. Test KAS (tKAS) has no value and can't be sold or swapped for real KAS.
- Pro on testnet is paid with test KAS. "🎁 Get free test credits" in the app pays a test top-up for you; no test coins needed.

- It's test software: no guarantees of any kind. Don't use it for anything you can't afford to lose.
- Never paste your secret words anywhere, including into feedback. Nobody from the team will DM you first or ask you to
  send KAS.
- Report bugs with "💬 Send feedback" in the app or by email to testnet@kaspachat.com. Security bugs: see the bounty terms.

## 3. Bug bounty terms

**Paid in real KAS** to a mainnet address you give us. Test KAS has no value.

| Severity | Examples | Bounty |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | Read someone else's messages, take over a name, spend someone's credits, leak an IP | 2,500 KAS |
| High | Messages lost or sent to the wrong person, payment taken but Pro not given, app crash on start | 1,000 KAS |
| Medium | Feature broken with a workaround, wrong timer behaviour, group bugs | 300 KAS |
| Low / UX | Confusing text, layout bugs, small annoyances | 100 KAS |

Also each week: best 3 testers 500 / 300 / 200 KAS. Seed testers: 100 KAS for a first valid report. Referral codes:
100 KAS when a new tester's first valid report includes your code (up to 5 rewards per person).

**Rules**
- The first valid report of a bug wins. Duplicates and spam get nothing. The KaspaChat team decides severity and validity.
- Security bugs: email testnet@kaspachat.com with the subject **SECURITY**. Never post them in the public group. Keep the
  details private until we say it's fixed. We reply within 24 hours and confirm severity within 48 hours.
- Payout address: send it from the same email you reported from. We repeat the first and last characters back to you to
  confirm before paying.
- Payouts go out in weekly batches, up to 5,000 KAS a week in total; anything above waits for the next batch. The total
  pool for this campaign is 10,000 KAS. The programme can end at any time; reports already confirmed are still paid.
- You handle your own taxes. No bounty for social-engineering the team, attacks on other people's devices, or anything
  that harms other testers or the network (no denial of service).
